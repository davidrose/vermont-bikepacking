The Night Before
Arrive in Burlington · Set up camp · Explore the waterfront
✦
North Beach Campground is Burlington's best-kept secret — 69 tent sites on a sandy Lake Champlain beach, minutes from downtown via a paved bike path. The sunsets over the Adirondacks are the warm-up act. Set up camp, walk to the waterfront for dinner, and wake up ready to ride. It's also just a short ride south to Airport Park where Day 1 begins.
PMarrive
North Beach CampgroundNight 0
Welcome to Burlington's very own beach resort! 69 majestic tent sites where the sand meets the water and your vacation begins. It's an urban campground but with that "I'm sleeping in the woods" vibe. The bike path is a literal conveyor belt right to downtown. Brace yourselves for Adirondack sunset views that will absolutely ruin all other sunsets for you. Hot showers to wash the travel off, and only a peppy 10-minute e-bike joyride to Airport Park. ☎ (802) 862-0942
Eve
Burlington WaterfrontDinner
Ditch the bikes and stroll down Church Street because real pants are optional but highly encouraged. Hit up Hen of the Wood if you want to eat like royalty who discovered farming, or smash an artisanal pie at American Flatbread while staring menacingly at the waterfront. Don't forget to loot the City Market co-op for organic snacks that will definitely get crushed in your panniers tomorrow.
1
The Lake Crossing
Airport Park → Brewster River Campground · ~42 mi · 8 mi Island Line · 14 mi road · 20 mi LVRT
✦
The causeway is three miles of marble rubble laid in 1899, hovering inches above Lake Champlain. The bike ferry at "The Cut" costs ~$8 RT and feels like a portal. Your first night is at a campground the owners discovered on a bikepacking trip during the pandemic — riverside tent sites, a 20-foot waterfall, fireflies in the meadow, and a Bernese Mountain Dog named Waffles who may greet you at the gate.
9:30a
Airport ParkStart
┼┼ Island Line Trail
Ditch the four-wheeled metal cage here at Airport Park in Colchester! It's completely free overnight parking in a neighborhood so sleepy, the raccoons are probably named "Reginald." The trail here soon turns to crushed stone heading onto the causeway. Fill the bottles, saddle up, lock the cars, and say goodbye to civilization.
10:00a20 min
Causeway + Bike FerryScenic
┼┼ Island Line → Ferry
The absolute main event. You will ride on a 3-mile rubble ribbon floating magically in the dead center of the lake. Prepare for 360-degree views that will make your eyeballs weep tears of immense joy. Ah, the "Local Motion" Bike Ferry. Because jumping an e-bike over a 200-foot water gap is frowned upon. The $8, 15-minute voyage runs continuously from 10am to 6pm. Yes, they take Apple Pay.
11:00a30 min ride
Hero's WelcomeSnack · 45 min
🛣 Road (US-2 through islands)
Behold the ultimate beacon of provisions: Hero's Welcome! This century-old general store is stuffed to the gills with sandwiches so massive they have their own gravity. The "Vermonter with Bacon" is practically a spiritual experience. Grab local cider, a borderline-concerning amount of pastries, and maybe a weird wooden duck souvenir. Eat triumphantly on the porch while judging the boaters. ☎ (802) 372-4161
1:30p45 min
St. AlbansResupply · 30 min
🛣 Road
St. Albans: The last bastion of modern convenience! This is your final chance to buy anything you forgot, like bug spray, gummy bears, or spare inner tubes. Stock up heavily on camp food and liquid fortitude before connecting to the LVRT via Missisquoi VRT. ⚠ Plan this connection segment in Ride with GPS.
4:00p1 hr 15 min
Brewster River CampgroundNight 1
┼┼ LVRT → 2 mi on Rt 108
Welcome to Brewster River Campground in Jeffersonville. You will find 13 incredibly chill riverside tent sites nestled right under the trees. There are zero RV hookups and zero generators allowed to ruin your vibe. The dark-sky stargazing is prime, and the shower house even has outlets so you can charge up the e-bikes and phones. ☎ (802) 391-0564
5:00p
Brewster River FallsSwim · Sunset
Once you set up the tents, practically fall out of your sleeping bag and walk to Brewster River Falls. It's a shockingly cold, crystal-clear swimming hole right below a 20-foot cascade. The campground owners literally tell people to "go chase the waterfall." Do not question them. Just plunge into the icy, majestic waters."
2
Into the Kingdom
Brewster River → New Discovery State Park · ~55 mi · 45 mi LVRT · 10 mi road
✦
This is the big day — 55 miles, but the LVRT does the work. The grade never exceeds 3%. You'll ride through 12-foot corn stalks, past the Fisher Covered Bridge (the last working covered railroad bridge in the country), and into the Northeast Kingdom. At Danville, peel south on VT-232 into Groton State Forest — 26,000 acres of CCC-era Vermont, where the lean-tos have stone fireplaces built by hand in the 1930s.
9:30adeparture

Early start — longest day. 2 mi to the LVRT trailhead at Cambridge Junction.

Wake up and smell the artisanal roasting! Two Son's Bakehouse in Hyde Park is perched right on our trail. Go absolutely feral on their breakfast sandwiches, inject some caffeine directly into your veins, and grab as many pastries as your pockets can hold. Today is powered purely by carbohydrates.

Lost Nation BrewingAlt StopKomoot Pick

Wait, an early detour for craft beer? Oh, absolutely. Lost Nation Brewing in Morrisville is an old warehouse masquerading as the outdoor mecca of Gose and IPAs. They serve Boyden Farm burgers that'll make you temporarily forget about the saddle sores. ⚠ Open Thu–Sun only, noon–7p. ☎ (802) 851-8041

Front Seat CoffeeLunch · 30 min
┼┼ LVRT → walk to Main St
Front Seat Coffee in Hardwick is your last chance to feel like a fancy city person. They have Cardamom Rose Lattes! Drink something exquisite and grab a scone, because after this, we are trading civilization for the deep, dark, beautiful woods of the Northeast Kingdom. Savor the espresso. ☎ (802) 472-7947

🛣 ~5 mi detour north from LVRT
The route begs us to take a detour to Hill Farmstead. It is literally rated the #1 brewery in the entire world. Yes, the WORLD. Are we going to climb a massive hill just for a glass of Edward pale ale? If the e-bikes have juice, the answer is a resounding, unapologetic "yes."
3:30p1 hr 15 min ride
New Discovery State ParkNight 2
┼┼ LVRT to Danville → 🛣 ~10 mi south on VT-232
Welcome to New Discovery State Park, buried deep in the silent embrace of Groton State Forest. Prepare for massive 1930s CCC-built stone fireplaces, lean-tos, and an unsettling amount of quiet. It is incredibly remote, so if anyone starts telling ghost stories, you are legally allowed to ride away into the night. ⚠ VT-232 has some hills — save energy. ☎ (802) 426-3042
3
The Easy Return
New Discovery → Elmore State Park · ~30 mi · 10 mi road · 15 mi LVRT · 5 mi road
✦
The payoff day. Retrace north to the LVRT, then let gravity do the work heading west. The 500 feet you climbed over two days now unspools behind you. Between Hyde Park and Johnson, pull off at Dog's Head Falls for a river swim. End at Elmore — "The Beauty Spot of Vermont" — where the CCC built a beach house in 1936 that still has a concession stand and boat rentals.
9:30adeparture
Depart New Discovery
🛣 VT-232 north ~10 mi → ┼┼ LVRT
Climb back out of Groton on VT-232 to rejoin the LVRT. It's a bit of an uphill battle to start the morning, but don't worry—it's basically all downhill once we hit the crushed gravel and point our compasses west.
11:00a1 hr ride
Lamoille River SwimmingSwim · 45 min
┼┼ LVRT (trailside)
Somewhere between Hyde Park and Johnson, we'll pull over at Dog's Head Falls. Yes, Dog's Head. There are sandy bottoms, freezing cold water, and zero lifeguards. Prepare to scream when the water hits your chest.
1:30p45 min ride
Elmore State ParkNight 3
🛣 VT-12, 5 mi south from LVRT
Ah, Elmore State Park. The locals call it "The Beauty Spot of Vermont," and they aren't kidding. Sandy beach, a 219-acre lake, and a beautiful 1930s beach house. ⚠ Absolutely NO electric hookups, so your e-bike better have some juice left. ☎ (802) 888-2982
2:30p
🏊 Lake Elmore Beach + 🗼 Fire TowerSwim + Hike
Waddle from the camp to the beach to cool down, and then if your legs somehow aren't absolute jelly, hike the 3-mile loop trail up to the Fire Tower (2,600 ft). The 360° golden-hour views from the top will be the postcard of the trip.

Back Across the Lake
Elmore → Airport Park · ~38 mi · 5 mi road · 20 mi LVRT/road · 8 mi Island Line
✦
The last day is the shortest and the sweetest. You already know the trail, the road, the causeway. But it hits different heading home — the lake is bigger when you're tired, and the moment you pull into Airport Park you'll feel the particular satisfaction of a loop closed. Three state parks, one riverside campground, zero RV hookups, and more Vermont than most people see in a lifetime.
